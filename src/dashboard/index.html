Install LIU App on iPhone

Install this application to your iPhone home screen to run in full-screen standalone mode without any Safari browser bars:

1
Tap the Share button ⎋ at the bottom of your Safari screen.
2
Scroll down the menu and tap Add to Home Screen ➕.
3
Tap Add in the top right corner. The LIU icon will appear on your phone home screen!
